01 · About me

Who I am

Curious · Analytical · Always learning

My journey began with a deep fascination for technology, especially Artificial Intelligence and data. That curiosity carried me into computer science and information security, where I built the technical foundation to work in the digital landscape, and then further into data and AI, developing skills in both engineering and analysis.

Professionally, the investment management industry gave me valuable insight into finance. Today I work in Senior Data Analytics at one of the Big Four firms, putting quantitative data, modeling and analysis to work. My experience includes IFRS 9 (financial instruments and expected credit loss) and IFRS 17 (insurance contracts) models.

Earlier, as an AI Research Assistant at a government research institute, I worked with machine learning and deep learning and co-developed Robot Sok, a Khmer-speaking robot.

I hold dual bachelor’s degrees: Computer Science from Preah Sihamoniraja Buddhist University, and Mathematics & Statistics from the Royal University of Phnom Penh. I’m now continuing with a Master of Information Technology Engineering at RUPP, focusing my research on Explainable AI: making machine learning models transparent and trustworthy. I also serve as a part-time researcher and industry expert lecturer at a higher education institute.

Khmer AI · Text-to-speech

Khmer VITS TTS

Code 🤗 Model

An end-to-end Khmer text-to-speech model I trained from scratch with VITS. One model speaks in 10 voices and reads Khmer script directly, with no phonemizer needed.

Trained on the open Khmer ASR Cultural Dataset by Digital Divide Data. Numbers are expanded into Khmer words automatically. Built for education and research, to help young Khmer AI developers.

  • Use it in a minuteDownload the pretrained model from Hugging Face. Runs on a normal CPU, including free Google Colab.
  • Train your ownA khmer-vits command line handles download, data preparation, training and synthesis.
